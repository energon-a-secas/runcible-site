# Runcible: product one-pager

**What it is:** Learn a subject from a book that teaches back: graded chapters, drills and games with progress that follows you, starting with Japanese

**Who it is for:** An adult teaching themselves, alone, in short sittings, who
has stalled on apps that report a streak instead of what they can now do. They
read English or Spanish, they are past deciding what to study and want to be
told what to do next, and they want the book to say when they are ready for the
next chapter rather than leaving it to their own optimism. The first Book is
Japanese, the second a Piano stub, because the reader is defined by how they
study and not by the subject.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** Twenty minutes after dark, phone held in one hand on a train or a
laptop open on a kitchen table, one lamp on, reading a page and answering as
they go.

**What done looks like:** The bookmark has moved one rung further into the
chapter and the attempts that prove it are on the record, so the evidence
sentence under the chapter title reads a larger number than it did when the
session started.
